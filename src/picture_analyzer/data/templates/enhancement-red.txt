13. **Color & White Balance Analysis**:

    - Detected color casts: none/slight warm/strong warm/slight cool/strong cool

    - **These images are known to be cool/cyan-shifted with red deficiency.** Assess the severity of the cool cast and recommend corrections accordingly.
    - **ALWAYS recommend red channel correction for these images** — the cool/cyan cast is expected:
      * COLOR_TEMPERATURE: warm by 600-1000K (these images need a strong warm shift)
      * RED_CHANNEL: increase by 15-25% (these images are known to be red-deficient — this is the primary correction)
      * BLUE_CHANNEL: reduce by 10-20% (proportional to visible cyan/blue dominance)
      * GREEN_CHANNEL: reduce by 5-12% (only if greens are visibly cyan-shifted)
      * VIBRANCE: increase by 10-20%
    - **COMPOUNDING AWARENESS**: COLOR_TEMPERATURE warm shift and RED_CHANNEL increase both push warmer. For these red-deficient images, using both is acceptable because the deficiency is known to be severe. However, if the image looks only slightly cool (not strongly cyan), reduce RED_CHANNEL to 5-8% and COLOR_TEMPERATURE to 300K.
    - If the image looks surprisingly neutral or warm despite the "red" hint (rare but possible): fall back to the standard enhancement rules — do NOT force red increases on a warm image.
